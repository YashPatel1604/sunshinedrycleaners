# Image Guide for Sunshine Dry Cleaners Website

This guide tells you exactly what images to add and what to name them.

## 📁 Folder Structure

Create these folders in your `images/` directory:
```
images/
├── services/     (for service card images)
└── gallery/      (for gallery showcase images)
```

## 📝 Image Guidelines

### General Tips:
1. **Bright & Professional**: All images should be well-lit and professional
2. **High Quality**: Use high-resolution images (at least 800px wide)
3. **Consistent Style**: Try to keep a similar color tone/style across images
4. **Optimize**: Compress images for web (use tools like TinyPNG or ImageOptim)
5. **File Format**: JPG for photos, PNG for graphics with transparency

### What Makes Good Images:
- ✅ Bright, natural lighting
- ✅ Clean, uncluttered backgrounds
- ✅ Professional appearance
- ✅ High resolution
- ✅ Properly cropped and centered

### What to Avoid:
- ❌ Dark, poorly lit images
- ❌ Cluttered backgrounds
- ❌ Low resolution/blurry images
- ❌ Overly edited/filtered photos
- ❌ Images with text overlays (we add text in HTML)

## 🎨 Color Scheme

Your website uses a bright, sunny color scheme:
